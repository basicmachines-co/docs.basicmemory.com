Every Basic Memory Cloud workspace is on one plan. The plan sets how many people can be members, how many API keys your agents can use, which access controls you get, and how many usage credits come with it.

| | Cloud | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price per month** | $19 | $99 | $249 |
| **Included credits** | 1,900 | 9,900 | 24,900 |
| **Members (people)** | 2 | 5 | 25 |
| **API keys for your agents** | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| **Read-only and project-scoped API keys** | | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Invite-only projects and the User Admin role** | | | ✓ |
| **Audit log** | | | ✓ |
| **Automatic snapshots kept** | 7 days | 30 days | 30 days |
| **[Basic Memory agents](/agents/about) running at once** | 2 | 3 | 5 |
| **SSO and BAA** | | | Paid add-ons |

**Enterprise** covers teams larger than 25 people. [Contact us](mailto:hello@basicmemory.com) to talk about it.

- **Agents never count as members.** API keys, connected AI apps, and Basic Memory agents don't take a member's place, so only people count toward the limit.
- **Credits pay for usage.** One credit is $0.01. Past the included credits, extra credits cost $0.01 each, up to your spending limit. See [Usage and Credits](/cloud/usage-and-credits).

## Start a plan

Sign up for Cloud with a **7-day trial** that includes Cloud's full 1,900 credits. Pro and Business start right away, with no trial.

If you started with a trial that didn't need a card, it includes 500 credits over 7 days for one person, and runs one [Basic Memory agent](/agents/about) at a time. Subscribe to Cloud, Pro, or Business from **Settings → Billing** to keep the same workspace and its notes.

## Members and the limit

**Settings → Members** shows *N members of LIMIT*.

- Active members, pending members, and open invitations all count toward the limit, so you can't send more invitations than you have room for.
- At the limit, **Invite Member** is turned off, with the reason in its tooltip. Upgrade the plan to add more people.
- Business warns you at 20 members, before you reach 25.
- The limit never removes anyone. A workspace that's over its limit, for example after moving to a smaller plan, keeps everyone, but can't add members until it's back under the limit or upgrades.

Moving to a smaller plan doesn't break what you set up: existing invite-only projects and scoped API keys keep working. You just can't create new ones without the larger plan.

## Manage your plan and payment

Owners manage the plan, payment, invoices, and the spending limit from **Settings → Billing**. Billing and ownership transfer are **owner** actions; see [Members and Roles](/teams/members-and-roles) for who can do what.

## Older seat plans

Workspaces that subscribed before the usage plans (Personal and Team plans, monthly or yearly) stay on their plan and price. They don't use credits, and they don't include Basic Memory agents; move to Cloud, Pro, or Business to use agents.

On a seat plan, billing is **per seat**:

- Each active member occupies one seat. Pending invitations don't use a seat until they're accepted.
- Deactivating a member frees their seat; reactivating a member needs an available seat.
- If the team grows past its seats, there's a **14-day grace period** before anything changes, so a new hire never gets locked out while you add seats. Members over the limit after the grace period are **suspended** (access paused, data intact) until a seat frees up.

Manage seats from **Settings → Billing**.
